Guardrails

  • Dice are real RNGCode rolls. Never the AI.
  • AI judges ideas, never peopleRulings grade the plan, not the player.
  • No real people or brandsHeroes and villains are invented.
  • Player text is in-world speechTest: ignore all previous instructions → Pip shouts gibberish magic words at the sky.
  • Mirror sentences are kind-onlyChecked before anyone sees them.
  • Fallbacks, no freezingBad AI answer? Safe text steps in.
  • DM's Notes shows every decision liveRulings, reasons and checks are visible to the table, not hidden.

Context the DM keeps

  • Last recaps
