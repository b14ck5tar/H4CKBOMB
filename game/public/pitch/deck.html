(About 12 seconds.) "Every round has five beats. A secret clue lands on each phone, so nobody can solve it alone and the table has to talk. Everyone pitches a plan. Muse rules on it, and you get one shot to argue your case. Then everyone rolls, and the dice are real random numbers in code, never the AI. And then your own words come alive in the story." If asked: Muse rulings are Clever (no roll), Solid, Risky, Absurd, Miracle. Friends can boost a plan, Catch a failed roll, and rescue a stuck hero.
