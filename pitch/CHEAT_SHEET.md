# Group Project: judge table cheat sheet (print me)

Judging Sun 2-5 PM, WEL. Stage pitch 5:30 PM, GDC 2.216. Partner hosts alone until the CD is back ~4:45.
TV: `groupproject-game.vercel.app/tv?judge=1&bots=1` (Quick · Odd news · Teens). Judge mode = 1 turn each, max 3,
then the finale. About 5 min from Start. Judge stories: the Busan canal shark, the €9M gold in a Dendermonde cellar
wall, the Norwich Batman.

## The 30-second pitch (say it while they scan)
"Last time you hung out with friends, how much of it was everyone on their own phone? Game nights fix that, but
someone has to run the game, so nobody plays. Here, Meta's Muse is the Dungeon Master. The TV is the table, your phone
is the controller. On your turn your friends send you ideas, and using one is your best move. You swipe to throw the
die, Muse paints what happens, and at the end you find out it was based on a true story. Scan this."

## The visit
1. QR card first, then the pitch. "It's a whole game in about five minutes. The best part is the last 30 seconds."
2. Heroes: "Muse is painting you as a cartoon. Your secret talent comes back at the end."
3. Start. Over the opener, once: "Muse wrote this and painted you in." Then quiet.
4. Turn 1: "Everyone else, send [A] an idea. Use a friend's and you get plus one." / "Which one made you laugh?"
5. "Swipe up to throw." / "Every number is its own outcome. Code rolls, never the AI."
6. Finale: "Everyone back one plan, not your own. Then everyone shakes at once."
7. Reveal: wait 3 s. "That's the real headline." "That's the talent you typed." "Take it home."
8. Close: "Your friends' ideas were your best move. Muse wrote, judged and painted every decision; code rolled the
   dice. It was based on a true story. And you just played it."
9. For Meta judges, last: "Every brain in this game is Meta's Muse: it writes, judges every plan, paints you in, and listens. The only thing it borrowed is a voice."

## 3 lines to memorize
1. "The one group project where everyone actually shows up."
2. "On your turn your friends pitch you ideas. Use one and they get the credit."
3. "Every story is based on a true story. You find out which one at the end."

## Top 5 judge questions
1. **What does the AI actually do?** "Muse Spark picks odd news that's safe and playable, writes the opening, judges
   every plan and writes every turn. Muse Image paints your heroes into each new scene. Press N: every call and why.
   Code rolls the dice. Take Muse away and there's no game."
2. **Is the story real?** "The starting event is a real article, or a player's own true story. Today's table plays a
   saved one from our checked library, so you never wait. Everything after the start is your plans plus Muse."
3. **What about dark news?** "A keyword block, then Muse scores every story: no deaths, injuries, crime, politics or
   health scares. It has to score 4 of 5 on 7 storyability checks. Grown-up stories, with hard limits the AI can't cross."
4. **How did you use AI to build it?** "Claude Code ran a team of agents: builders, testers, reviewers. They watched
   our playtest videos frame by frame; our worst run is why we rebuilt the story around players' choices. A better
   rubric took Muse Spark from 5 of 8 fair rulings to 8 of 8."
5. **What's next?** "Campaign mode: your heroes carry over into the next story, like real D&D. Then Our stories as the
   main mode, and an opt-in Real World mode with guardrails."

## If something breaks
- **Muse slow or down:** say nothing. Canned lines fill in, the old painting stays. All fallbacks? Play the video.
- **Narrator silent:** "Tonight the narrator is shy." Text still shows.
- **Wi-Fi down:** the game pauses by itself. Laptop to the phone hotspot, judges rescan. Hotspot down too: play the
  backup video (laptop + phone) and talk over it; hand out the QR card.
- **Freeze:** F5 on the TV, everyone rescans. Twice = video.
- **Judge in a hurry:** 30-second pitch + deck reveal slide + QR card.

## HackTX general judging (Best Overall)
- **Technology:** real-time multi-phone rooms run by the TV; 4 Muse jobs (judge plans, write turns, paint your heroes
  into each scene, transcribe) + the MCP connector; a live news pipeline with an AI storyability gate; code checks
  every AI change; crypto dice; generate-ahead so nobody waits >5 s; music made in code. *Point at:* DM's Notes (N),
  the how-it-works diagram.
- **Design:** Neon Ink art, Jackbox-style lobby, phone UI, every AI call explainable. *Point at:* lobby, painting, phone.
- **Completion:** plays end to end on the live link; every AI call has a fallback. *Point at:* their own game.
- **Learning (both of you):** "AI needs a director: left alone it drifts and writes stories you can't follow, so our
  engine gives Muse rules and code checks every move." "Build the prototype fast: our first playtest found problems no
  plan would have." "AI makes things look good fast; making them feel good took playtesting." "First hackathon:
  stressful, fun, worth it." (Never say "AI is bad at stories".)

## Never say
"4 minutes" · "this week's news" (at the table) · "nothing is saved" · "20 cents" as fact ·
danger meter, Catch, Boost, Mirror, clues, Convince the DM, Hope · "Play it now" until the deploy is checked.
