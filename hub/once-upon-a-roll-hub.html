    How a round plays

    PitchEveryone types or says an action on their phone (25 s).
    VerdictThe AI rates each idea: Clever +3, Solid, Risky −3, Absurd −6, Miracle.
    PickThe party votes on the best idea (not your own).
    RollThe winner rolls a d20. 1 is catastrophe, 20 is legend.
    • Catch! Spend your one Heart to reroll a friend’s failure.
    • Rescue Round. A nat 1 leaves a hero in peril; friends pitch rescues and the hero in peril picks their savior.
    • Final Roll. Everyone rolls together; the best die plus the party’s Hope decides the ending.
    • The reveal. The storybook tears away into a newspaper clipping: “This tale was inspired by real events.”
    • Bookshelf. Every tale is saved to the party’s shelf, so next week is Chapter 2.
