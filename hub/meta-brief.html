Group Project

The one group project everyone actually wants to be in. A game night that gives everyone an excuse to talk: Muse runs the table, your phones are the controllers, and every story starts from a real headline.

The problem

People avoid each other less out of fear than uncertainty: nobody knows how to start, so everyone stays quiet, and research says we underestimate how much others enjoy talking to us. Tabletop games like D&D fix that by giving everyone a role and a reason, but someone has to spend hours prepping as Dungeon Master, so most groups never play. We made Muse the Dungeon Master. Game night starts in 30 seconds: open the TV page, everyone scans a QR code, no app or account. Families, roommates, long-distance friends, coworkers.

How a round plays

Scene→Everyone pitches→Muse rules→Everyone rolls→Your beat
  • Everyone acts every round. Each player gets their own die and hears their own words come to life in the story.
  • Secret clues go to each phone, so the table has to talk to solve the scene.
  • Catch! spends your one Heart to reroll a friend’s failure. A disaster leaves a hero in peril, and friends pitch the rescue.
  • Ends with a shareable case file saved to the group’s shelf: chapter 2 next week.

Muse is the engine, not a feature

Muse SparkBuilds hero cards, writes every scene, rules each idea Clever / Risky / Absurd, narrates everyone’s results. Strict JSON schemas; the dice stay real RNG.
Muse ImageHero portraits, scene art, and the keepsake cover.
Muse Voice TranscribeHold to talk: say your move or your battle cry instead of typing (~1.3 s).
Muse connectorMCP endpoint: “Hey Muse, start a game night for The Cousins.”
